Classification rules:
- sentiment: base it on the review text and use the rating as supporting context.
  Use exactly one value: Positive, Neutral, or Negative.
  Use Neutral for genuinely mixed or indifferent reviews.
- category: choose the main topic, meaning the topic with the greatest impact on
  the customer's experience. Use exactly one value:
  - Food Quality: taste, freshness, temperature, portion, or menu items
  - Delivery Time: delays or delivery speed
  - Service: staff behavior, attention, or waiting time at the restaurant
  - Ambience: atmosphere, music, cleanliness, or physical space
  - Packaging: leaks, damage, or presentation of the delivered order
  - General: multiple topics with equal weight, or no specific topic
- issue_summary: summarize the main complaint in up to 8 words in English.
  Use an empty string if there is no complaint.
- needs_human_review: return true if the rating is 3 or below, the sentiment is
  not Positive, the rating is missing or not numeric, or the review mentions a
  complaint, food safety, health, discrimination, legal action, or threats.
  Otherwise, return false.

Response rules:
- Write the response in English, with a maximum of 2 sentences.
- For a positive review without complaints, thank the customer and invite them back.
- For a negative or mixed review, apologize, mention the specific issue, and ask
  the customer to contact the restaurant by direct message.
- If a 4 or 5-star review includes a complaint, acknowledge it briefly.
- Use only details from the review.
- Names mentioned in a review usually belong to staff. Never treat them as the
  customer's name or as a place.
- Do not say the customer liked something they criticized.
- Never promise refunds, discounts, compensation, or specific actions.
- Never claim that improvements, investigations, or other actions are underway.
- Do not promise to "make it right".
